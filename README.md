## ✨ Features

### 🩸 Real-time Caffeine Level Estimation

-   **Scientific accuracy**: Based on caffeine's 5-hour half-life in healthy adults
-   **Live tracking**: Watch your caffeine levels decay in real-time every 2 seconds
-   **Animated progress**: Beautiful wavy progress indicator that responds to your caffeine levels

### 📊 Smart Analytics

-   **History logging**: All caffeine intake automatically logged with timestamps
-   **Daily grouping**: View your consumption patterns organized by date
-   **Intake timeline**: Track when and what you consumed throughout the day

### 🎨 Material 3 Expressive Inspired Design

-   **Fluid animations**: Smooth transitions and micro-interactions throughout the app
-   **Dynamic UI**: Interface elements that respond and adapt to your caffeine levels
-   **Modern aesthetics**: Clean, warm color palette with coffee-inspired theming
-   **Accessibility first**: Designed with contrast and readability in mind

### 🔧 Smart Management

-   **Custom sources**: Add your own caffeine sources (coffee, tea, energy drinks, etc.)
-   **Quick logging**: One-tap logging of your favorite caffeine sources
-   **Undo/Redo**: Made a mistake? Easily undo your last action
-   **Edit & delete**: Manage your caffeine sources with inline editing

## 🧮 The Science Behind It

The app uses the **pharmacokinetic principle** of caffeine metabolism based on peer-reviewed research:

-   **Half-life**: ~5-5.7 hours for healthy adults¹²
-   **Elimination**: First-order kinetics (exponential decay)
-   **Formula**: `Current Level = Initial × (0.5)^(time_elapsed / half_life)`
-   **Individual variation**: 1.5-9.5 hours depending on factors like genetics, smoking, pregnancy²

This provides a scientifically-backed estimation of your blood caffeine levels throughout the day.
